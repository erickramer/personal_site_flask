About

I'm Eric Kramer — a data scientist who used to be a doctor. These days I work at OpenAI, helping teams make sense of complex systems and turn data into understanding. Before that, I spent years in hospitals, learning how people make decisions under pressure and how evidence and empathy can work together.

I've lived in France, Israel, and Bhutan, experiences that taught me to notice how differently people approach the same problems — and how much perspective matters when building anything meant to serve others.

Now I live in San Francisco's Noe Valley with my wife, Paige, our two sons, Jude and Rhett, and our cats, Moose and Boaz. Life is happily full — early mornings, park trips, bedtime stories, and the occasional quiet hour to read, or plan our next family adventure.
